Complete Guide to the 5 Classic Body Shapes

Every body has unique, natural proportions. Using a Body Shape Calculator helps you understand how your shoulders/bust, waist, and hips relate to one another—making outfit styling, tailored shopping, and partner fitness routines effortless.

⏳ Hourglass Shape

Bust and hip measurements are nearly equal (within 5% of each other) with a well-defined, narrower natural waist.

🍐 Pear (Triangle) Shape

Hips and thighs are noticeably wider than the shoulders and bust, creating a graceful lower-body curve and defined waist.

🔻 Inverted Triangle

Shoulders or bust are broader than the hips, giving a strong, athletic upper-body silhouette.

📏 Rectangle (Athletic)

Bust, waist, and hip measurements are closely aligned, forming a balanced, streamlined vertical frame.

🍎 Apple (Round) Shape

Weight is carried primarily around the midsection/waist relative to slender hips and legs.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do I measure myself accurately for the Body Shape Calculator?

Use a flexible measuring tape held snug (not tight) parallel to the floor: measure around the fullest part of your bust/chest, the narrowest part of your natural waist (just above the navel), and the widest part of your hips.

Can I use centimeters instead of inches?

Yes! Body shape classification is based on proportional ratios and percentage differences between your bust, waist, and hips, so both inches and centimeters produce the exact same result.

What is a healthy Waist-to-Hip Ratio (WHR)?

According to general wellness guidelines, a waist-to-hip ratio of 0.85 or lower for women and 0.90 or lower for men is associated with balanced cardiovascular wellness.
