How the Visual Body Shape & BMI Checker Works

Understanding your body proportions and Body Mass Index (BMI) is a simple, visual way to track personal wellness goals—whether solo or as a couple staying active together. Unlike plain number calculators, the DuoOrbit Visual Silhouette Checker translates your height-to-weight ratio into an immediate visual outline.

Understanding Your Silhouette Categories

Slender Silhouette (BMI under 18.5): Indicates a light, agile frame. Nutrient-rich meals and light resistance exercises help build steady energy and strength.

Balanced Silhouette (BMI 18.5 – 24.9): Represents balanced height-to-weight proportions. Regular walking, stretching, and mindful nutrition help maintain optimal vitality.

Curvy & Athletic Frame (BMI 25.0 – 29.9): Reflects a grounded, sturdy build. Enjoyable partner activities like hiking, cycling, or dancing support cardiovascular health.

Full Silhouette (BMI 30.0 and above): Highlights a fuller frame where consistent, low-impact movement and sustainable daily habits bring lasting wellness benefits.
