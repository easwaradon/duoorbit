Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Welcome to DuoOrbit (accessible at duoorbit.com). Your privacy is of paramount importance to us. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information collected and recorded by DuoOrbit and how we use it, specifically catering to visitors across the United States, Europe (GDPR), and globally.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

DuoOrbit operates as an entertaining couple compatibility tool. We respect your anonymity:

  • We do not require account registration or login.
  • Quiz responses and generated results are processed locally on your browser or handled anonymously and are not stored permanently on personal profile databases.

2. Log Files and Web Analytics

Like many websites, DuoOrbit utilizes standard log files and privacy-focused web analytics. The information gathered includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamps, referring/exit pages, and number of clicks. This data is not linked to any personally identifiable information and is solely used to analyze trends, administer the site, and improve user experience.

3. Cookies and Advertising Partners (Google AdSense)

DuoOrbit may serve third-party advertisements via Google AdSense and other advertising networks. These third-party vendors use cookies, web beacons, and similar tracking technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other sites on the internet.

  • Google's DART Cookie: Google uses cookies to serve targeted advertisements to our site visitors based on their visit to duoorbit.com and other websites across the web. Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalized advertising by visiting AboutAds.info.

4. General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) Compliance

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA), you have specific data protection rights. DuoOrbit aims to take reasonable steps to allow you to correct, amend, delete, or limit the use of any personal data. You have the right to request access, rectification, or erasure of any data we hold regarding you (if applicable).

5. California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) Compliance

Under the CCPA, California consumers have the right to request that a business disclose what personal data it collects, request the deletion of personal data, and request that a business not sell their personal data. DuoOrbit does not sell personal information to third parties.

6. Third-Party Privacy Policies

DuoOrbit's Privacy Policy does not apply to other advertisers or external websites. We advise you to consult the respective Privacy Policies of third-party ad servers or networks for more detailed information regarding their practices.

7. Contact Information

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, please contact us via email at: support@duoorbit.com
